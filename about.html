Welcome to the 206 words.

About

Hi, I am Ninjin and I made this website for self-reflection and self-exploration. I was feeling lost and listening to some of my favorite songs, when I wrote down I am made up of 206 words. Afterwards, I tried writing down each 206 words on Google Docs. But then I thought it could be more fun and creative, so I decided to turn it into this project.

What does 206 words mean?

An adult body has about 206 bones. So I wondered: what if each bone held one meaningful word? Over a lifetime, we would gather 206 words, one for each bone -- loved ones' names, dreams, memories, and the things we treasure the most.

(206 is the textbook average, not a rule. Many bodies have more or fewer bones. The skeleton here is only one version of a body, and I know it doesn’t reflect everyone. Your words belong here regardless of the number.)

Do I have to write exactly 206 words?

No you don't. There is no right or wrong. Write what feels right to you.

What happens to my writing?

Your words are saved only in your browser, on your device. This means you can come back anytime on the same device and browser and continue where you left off. This allows your writing to stay private. Nothing is sent to a server, so no one else can see it.

Warning: Clearing your browser data will permanently erase your words from this site. To keep them, use Download to save your skeleton or a single bone as a PNG, JPG, or SVG image. You can look back at it anytime, but it can not be uploaded to restore your words here.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Unfortunately, no. Everything is saved locally on your device. I decided this way because I wanted to value privacy and safety.

How do I get started?

If you mean from the technical side, you can start by clicking on a bone then writing a word in it or coloring them.

But if you mean from the non-technical side, write a few words, take a walk, go to your favorite cafe, listen to music, talk to a friend and then come back the next day. There is no rush to fill out each word or finish everything within a day.

And when you feel that you are ready to share it or save it, you can hit the download button. If you are sharing it online, make sure to hide any sensitive information.
