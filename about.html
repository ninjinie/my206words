Welcome to the 206 words.

Hi, I am Ninjin and I made this website while feeling lost and listening to some of my favorite music. At the time, I had the habit of writing down whatever words came into my mind. So I wrote down "I am made up of X words." Then I searched how many bones there were in the human body. Plugged in. After that, I wrote down a few of the words in a simple Google Doc. But then I thought I could write them on each bone, and that turned into this project.

I think how I would want someone to use this website would be: write a few words, take a walk, go to your favorite cafe, write; do something that you love, then write; write down a memory, then write down a word; write down a word, then try to think what it means to you. Listen to music. Talk to a friend. And then come back the next day. I don't think there is any rush to fill out each word or finish everything within a day. And I am not even sure if I will be able to find 206 meaningful words in this lifetime, but I hope I do.

While this won't completely help you find out who you are, I hope it can give a sense of direction at least and bring some comfort.

Lastly, to whoever ends up reading this, I hope things will get better. I know you are doing your best.

Best,
Ninjin
